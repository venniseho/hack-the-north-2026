Project:

Problem

Scam e-commerce sites often attract shoppers with unusually cheap deals, fake reviews, misleading product claims, and AI-generated social proof. It can be difficult to tell whether a store or product is legitimate before purchasing.

Solution

Build a browser extension that acts as an AI-powered scam detector for online shopping.
When a user visits an e-commerce site, the extension detects the page and lets them run a scam check. It researches the store and product across multiple signals, then produces a trust score with an evidence-based breakdown, including:
Reddit discussions or reports flagging the store or product as suspicious
Trustpilot and other reputable third-party reviews
AI-generated or suspicious product reviews
AI-generated social media posts used to promote the product
Product materials, specifications, and claims that appear inconsistent or misleading
Not having tags on instagram and other social media (other people promoting them)
